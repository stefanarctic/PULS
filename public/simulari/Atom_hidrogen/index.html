Formule cheie

Bohr: \(E_n = -\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}\) eV
\(\Delta E = E_{n_f}-E_{n_i}\), \(E_\gamma = |\Delta E|\)
\(\lambda = \dfrac{hc}{E_\gamma}\)
\(E_\gamma = hf\)
\(f = \dfrac{c}{\lambda}\)
Rydberg: \(\dfrac{1}{\lambda} = R\left(\dfrac{1}{n_f^2}-\dfrac{1}{n_i^2}\right)\)
\(R = 1.0973731568508 \times 10^7 m^{-1}\)
de Broglie: \(\lambda_{dB}=\dfrac{h}{p}\), \(2\pi r = n\lambda_{dB}\)
\(p = mv\)
Schrödinger: \(|\psi|^2 \Rightarrow\) densitate de probabilitate
\(E = \dfrac{h^2}{8m}\nabla^2\psi + V\psi\)
\(V = -\dfrac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r}\)
